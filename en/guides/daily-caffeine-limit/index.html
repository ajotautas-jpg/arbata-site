How much caffeine a day is safe?

The European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) assessed caffeine safety in 2015. Here are its limits and what they mean in cups.

EFSA limits

  • Adults: up to 400 mg a day from all sources.
  • At one time: up to 200 mg.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: up to 200 mg a day.
  • Children and teenagers: about 3 mg per kilogram of body weight a day (for example about 120 mg for a 40 kg child).

How long caffeine stays in the body

The half-life of caffeine is usually 3–7 hours (we use about 5), so an afternoon cup can still act in the evening. Smoking, medication, pregnancy and genetics change this.

General information, not medical advice. If you are pregnant, take medication or have a health condition, talk to your doctor.
