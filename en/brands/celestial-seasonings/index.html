Frequently asked questions

Does Celestial Seasonings tea have more caffeine than coffee?

Usually not: a cup of tea typically has less caffeine than a cup of filter coffee (about 96 mg), but it depends on the product, amount and steeping time.

Is the caffeine amount always the same?

No. It depends on the batch, the amount of leaf and steeping time, which is why we show ranges. Only the manufacturer can confirm an exact figure.
