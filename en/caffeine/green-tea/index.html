How much caffeine is in green tea?

Green tea (237 ml) usually contains 11–29 mg of caffeine, about 20 mg on average.

Based on 2 g of dry leaf, 237 ml of water, about 5 min of steeping.

How caffeine changes with steeping

Longer steeping usually brings more caffeine. Figures for 2 g of tea.

Steeping timeCaffeine per cup
2 min7–19 mg
3 min9–25 mg
4 min10–27 mg
5 min11–29 mg

What about the evening?

After 6 hours roughly 3–16 mg of caffeine is still in the body (half-life 3–7 h). Sensitivity varies, so treat this as a guide, not a rule.

Frequently asked questions

How much caffeine is in green tea?

Green tea (237 ml) usually contains 11–29 mg of caffeine, about 20 mg on average. Based on 2 g of dry leaf, 237 ml of water, about 5 min of steeping.

Does green tea have more caffeine than filter coffee?

No, on average less: about 21% of the caffeine in a cup of filter coffee.

How many cups of green tea can I drink a day?

For adults EFSA gives up to 400 mg of caffeine a day from all sources. From this drink alone that is roughly 20 cups, if nothing else adds caffeine. In pregnancy the limit is 200 mg a day.
