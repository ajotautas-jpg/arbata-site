How much caffeine is in matcha?

Matcha (200 ml) usually contains 38–89 mg of caffeine, about 63 mg on average.

Based on 2 g of powder, 200 ml of water (the whole leaf is consumed).

How much depends on grams

With matcha you consume the whole leaf, so the number of grams matters most.

GramsCaffeine per cup
1 g19–44 mg
2 g38–89 mg
3 g57–133 mg

What about the evening?

After 6 hours roughly 10–49 mg of caffeine is still in the body (half-life 3–7 h). Sensitivity varies, so treat this as a guide, not a rule.

Frequently asked questions

How much caffeine is in matcha?

Matcha (200 ml) usually contains 38–89 mg of caffeine, about 63 mg on average. Based on 2 g of powder, 200 ml of water (the whole leaf is consumed).

Does matcha have more caffeine than filter coffee?

No, on average less: about 66% of the caffeine in a cup of filter coffee.

How many cups of matcha can I drink a day?

For adults EFSA gives up to 400 mg of caffeine a day from all sources. From this drink alone that is roughly 6 cups, if nothing else adds caffeine. In pregnancy the limit is 200 mg a day.
