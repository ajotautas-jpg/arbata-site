How much caffeine is in an energy drink?

Energy drink (237 ml) contains about 79 mg of caffeine.

Standard serving: 237 ml.

What about the evening?

After 6 hours roughly 20–43 mg of caffeine is still in the body (half-life 3–7 h). Sensitivity varies, so treat this as a guide, not a rule.

Frequently asked questions

How much caffeine is in an energy drink?

Energy drink (237 ml) contains about 79 mg of caffeine. Standard serving: 237 ml.

Does energy drink have more caffeine than filter coffee?

No, on average less: about 82% of the caffeine in a cup of filter coffee.

How many cups of energy drink can I drink a day?

For adults EFSA gives up to 400 mg of caffeine a day from all sources. From this drink alone that is roughly 5 cups, if nothing else adds caffeine. In pregnancy the limit is 200 mg a day.
