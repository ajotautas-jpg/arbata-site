How much caffeine is in decaf coffee?

Decaf coffee (237 ml) contains about 1 mg of caffeine.

Standard serving: 237 ml.

Frequently asked questions

How much caffeine is in decaf coffee?

Decaf coffee (237 ml) contains about 1 mg of caffeine. Standard serving: 237 ml.

Does decaf coffee have more caffeine than filter coffee?

No, on average less: about 1% of the caffeine in a cup of filter coffee.

How many cups of decaf coffee can I drink a day?

For adults EFSA gives up to 400 mg of caffeine a day from all sources. From this drink alone that is roughly more than 20 cups, if nothing else adds caffeine. In pregnancy the limit is 200 mg a day.
