How it is made

Made from Aspalathus linearis, a broom-like legume that grows in South Africa's Western Cape, mostly in the Cederberg mountains. It is not the tea plant (Camellia sinensis), so rooibos counts as a herbal infusion. The leaves are usually oxidised, which gives the reddish-brown colour. Green (unoxidised) rooibos is also made, but it costs more to produce.

Taste

Oxidation gives the red-brown colour and enhances the flavour, so oxidised and green rooibos differ. The sources give no more detailed taste description, so we do not write one.

How to brew

Steeped in hot water, like black tea. We found no exact temperature or time in the sources, so follow the packaging.

Frequently asked questions

How much caffeine is in rooibos?

The sources describe rooibos as a caffeine-free herbal infusion: 0 mg per cup.

How do you brew rooibos?

Steeped in hot water, like black tea. We found no exact temperature or time in the sources, so follow the packaging.

Does rooibos count towards the daily caffeine limit?

No. Rooibos is described as caffeine-free, so it does not add to your daily caffeine total (the EFSA limit for adults is 400 mg a day).
