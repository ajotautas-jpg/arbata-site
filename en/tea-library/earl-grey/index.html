Earl Grey: caffeine, brewing and taste

Earl Grey is usually black tea, so the base tea decides the caffeine.

At a glance

Water temperaturesee packaging
Steeping time2–3 min

Caffeine

In our data black tea has 14–48 mg per cup. Versions on green, white or oolong tea differ, and a rooibos base has no caffeine. The source gives no separate Earl Grey caffeine figure.

How it is made

Earl Grey is a tea blend flavoured with oil of bergamot, a citrus oil from the bergamot orange rind. The base is traditionally black tea, such as Chinese keemun, sometimes with lapsang souchong for a smoky note. Green and oolong versions exist too. Many commercial blends use other natural or artificial flavours instead of bergamot oil.

Variants and taste

Bergamot gives the main flavour note. Variants: Lady Grey (with cornflower or citrus, a Twinings trademark), Russian Earl Grey (citrus peel and lemongrass), French Earl Grey (rose petals), Earl Grey Crème (lavender and vanilla).

How to brew

About 2–3 minutes; some recommend up to 5 minutes for more citrus aroma. Over-steeping makes it bitter. Sources do not agree on temperature.
