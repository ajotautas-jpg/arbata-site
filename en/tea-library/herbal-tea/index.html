Herbal teas: caffeine, brewing and taste

Most herbal infusions contain no caffeine (the general figure is 0 mg per cup).

Caffeine

There are exceptions: guarana, yaupon and yerba mate do contain caffeine. Products made in shared factories can also be contaminated slightly, so check the ingredients.

How it is made

Herbal teas (tisanes) are made by steeping or simmering herbs, spices or other plant material in water. They contain no tea leaves, so they are not true tea: they do not come from the tea plant (Camellia sinensis). Examples are chamomile and peppermint.

Taste

Taste depends on the plant. The sources give no general description.

How to brew

Hot or boiling water is poured over the plant parts and left to steep. Temperature and time vary with the plant part, so follow the packaging. Cold-water brewing takes several hours.

Frequently asked questions

How much caffeine is in herbal teas?

Most herbal infusions contain no caffeine (the general figure is 0 mg per cup).

How do you brew herbal tea?

Hot or boiling water is poured over the plant parts and left to steep. Temperature and time vary with the plant part, so follow the packaging. Cold-water brewing takes several hours.

Are all herbal teas caffeine-free?

Not all. Most are, but guarana, yaupon and yerba mate contain caffeine. Check the ingredients, especially on blends sold as "energy" or "boost". Some herbs can interact with medicines, so if you take medication it is worth asking a pharmacist or doctor.
