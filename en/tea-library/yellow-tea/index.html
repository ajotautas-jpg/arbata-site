How it is made

Yellow tea follows the green tea process with one extra step, called "sweltering": warm, damp leaves are sealed in a container and gently heated for about 6–8 hours at close to body temperature. This turns them yellow and helps remove green tea's grassy smell. In China it is made in Hunan, Anhui, Sichuan, Zhejiang, Guizhou, Guangdong and Hubei.

Taste

Sweltering changes the amino acids and polyphenols, giving a brisk yet mellow taste. Chinese yellow tea gives a yellowish-green liquor.
