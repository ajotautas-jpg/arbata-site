A slow weekend morning: a tea tasting at home

At the weekend pick one tea from the library, brew it for the recommended time, smell it, taste it and write down the flavour. Over a few weekends you will find your favourite coffee replacement.

What you need

  • One tea from the library
  • A timer
  • A notebook or the diary

How to make it

  1. Choose a tea: black, green, oolong, white or another from the library.
  2. Brew it at the library temperature and time (a timer helps).
  3. Smell, taste without milk and note the flavour.
  4. Next weekend try another kind and compare.

How much caffeine

In our data black tea has 14–48 mg, green 11–29 mg, matcha 38–89 mg. Brewing time changes caffeine; the library tables show how.

Tips

  • Start with one kind per weekend: too many flavours at once is confusing.
  • A diary helps you remember which tea you liked.

FAQ

How do I pick a tea to replace coffee?

Try a few kinds one at a time and note the taste: it is very personal.

How long does a tasting take?

About 15–20 minutes per tea.
