When you crave coffee: a 10-minute control ritual

When a coffee craving hits, wait 10 minutes with a glass of water or a warm caffeine-free drink, write it down, and only then decide. That separates habit from real need.

What you need

  • A glass of water or rooibos / herbal tea
  • A diary or tracker
  • 10 minutes

How to make it

  1. Stop and tell yourself: first I will wait 10 minutes.
  2. Drink water or warm caffeine-free tea.
  3. Write down the time and how you feel (diary).
  4. After 10 minutes decide. If you have coffee, do it knowingly and count it in your daily limit.

How much caffeine

Adult daily limit: 400 mg, 200 mg at one time (EFSA). A cup of filter coffee has 80–107 mg, black tea 14–48 mg.

Tips

  • In the first days of cutting caffeine you may get a headache: see the article on withdrawal.
  • Set a daily limit with the planner and follow it with the tracker.

FAQ

Why do I crave coffee?

Often it is habit and caffeine dependence. Our article on withdrawal explains what to expect.

Do I need to quit at once?

No, most people find gradual reduction easier.
