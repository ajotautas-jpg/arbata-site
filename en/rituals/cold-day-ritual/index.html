A cold-day ritual: warm tea instead of the third coffee

When it is cold, swap the third coffee for warm caffeine-free tea (rooibos, herbal), hold the cup in both hands and drink without hurrying. A cup of filter coffee has 80–107 mg; rooibos has no caffeine.

What you need

  • Rooibos or herbal tea
  • A big cup
  • A spot by the window or a warm corner

How to make it

  1. Brew a larger cup than usual.
  2. Add a cinnamon stick, lemon peel or honey if you like.
  3. Sit and drink without your phone; the warmth of the cup is part of the ritual.
  4. Repeat at the same time, for example after lunch.

How much caffeine

Rooibos has no caffeine and most herbal teas do not either, but not all: check the pack. Filter coffee 80–107 mg, black tea 14–48 mg.

Tips

  • We do not claim tea will warm or heal you: this is a warm-drink pause idea.
  • For a warm drink with milk try rooibos latte.

FAQ

What can I drink instead of coffee on a cold day?

Warm rooibos or herbal tea or a rooibos latte; they give no caffeine.

Are herbal teas always caffeine-free?

Usually, but not always: blends may include tea with caffeine. Check the pack.
