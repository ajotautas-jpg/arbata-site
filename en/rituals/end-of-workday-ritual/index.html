An end-of-workday ritual: tea that closes the day

Have your last caffeinated cup several hours before the evening (half-life 3–7 hours), and close the workday with a warm caffeine-free drink and a 5-minute walk.

What you need

  • Rooibos or herbal tea
  • 5 minutes for a walk
  • The last-cup tool

How to make it

  1. Check when you had your last caffeinated cup (last-cup tool).
  2. Brew a caffeine-free tea and drink it away from work.
  3. Walk for 5 minutes or step outside.
  4. Write down tomorrow’s first task and close the computer.

How much caffeine

Black tea 14–48 mg, green 11–29 mg, a cup of filter coffee 80–107 mg. Rooibos has no caffeine. Caffeine has a half-life of 3–7 hours.

Tips

  • A ritual is no substitute for rest: if sleep is poor for long, talk to a doctor.
  • Set a daily time for your last caffeinated cup.

FAQ

When should I stop caffeine in the day?

It depends on the person; caffeine stays for a half-life of 3–7 hours. The last-cup tool helps estimate.

Does tea after work affect sleep?

With caffeine it can; caffeine-free (rooibos, herbal) usually does not.
