A coffee break swap: what to drink instead of afternoon coffee

Swap the afternoon coffee for a warm caffeine-free drink (rooibos, herbal tea) or a cold brew. Caffeine has a half-life of 3–7 hours, so late coffee is still partly in your body in the evening.

What you need

  • Two or three drinks you can prepare at work
  • A cup you like
  • 10 minutes for a break

How to make it

  1. Pick 2–3 drinks with no or little caffeine: rooibos, herbal tea, cold brew green tea.
  2. Prepare ahead so you do not walk to the coffee machine.
  3. Take a short walk or chat with a colleague while you drink: a break, not just a drink.
  4. Use the last-cup tool to see when to stop caffeine.

How much caffeine

Rooibos and most herbal teas contain no caffeine; black tea has 14–48 mg, green 11–29 mg. With a half-life of 3–7 hours, caffeine drunk after lunch can remain into the evening.

Tips

  • If the afternoon is hard, drop the last coffee first, not the first.
  • Cold brew tea suits a workday: made the evening before.

FAQ

When should I stop caffeine in the day?

It depends on the person; caffeine stays for a half-life of 3–7 hours. The last-cup tool helps estimate.

What can replace afternoon coffee?

Rooibos latte, herbal tea or cold brew tea.
