A morning ritual without coffee: starting the day with tea

Replace only the morning cup first: a cup of filter coffee has 80–107 mg, black tea 14–48 mg, green tea 11–29 mg, matcha 38–89 mg. The same cup, the same time and 10 minutes without a screen turn a routine into a ritual.

What you need

  • The same cup every day
  • A tea of your choice (black or matcha to start)
  • 10 minutes at the start of the morning

How to make it

  1. Find out how much caffeine you get now: the caffeine calculator shows the daily total.
  2. Replace the first cup: it is usually the easiest, because it is where the habit is strongest.
  3. Drink at the same time from the same cup; add 10 minutes without your phone if you can.
  4. After a week replace the second cup. For a gradual plan use the planner tool.

How much caffeine

EFSA says up to 400 mg of caffeine a day and up to 200 mg at one time is safe for adults. 200 mg is about 6 cups of black tea at our typical value (31 mg). Caffeine has a half-life of 3–7 hours.

Tips

  • In the first days you may get a headache or sleepiness: see the article on caffeine withdrawal.
  • Do not aim for zero at once: a gradual reduction is easier.

FAQ

Where do I start swapping coffee for tea?

With one cup in the morning. When it becomes a habit, swap the next.

Which tea is closest to coffee?

By caffeine, matcha (38–89 mg) or yerba mate; the taste differs.
