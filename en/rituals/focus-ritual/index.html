A focus ritual for work: one cup and a timer

Before important work have one cup of green tea (11–29 mg) or matcha (38–89 mg), set a timer (for example 25–50 minutes) and put your phone away. More cups add caffeine, not ritual.

What you need

  • Green tea or matcha
  • A timer
  • A place without notifications

How to make it

  1. Prepare the tea before you start, not during.
  2. Set the timer and pick one task.
  3. When it ends, take a short break: stand up, stretch, drink water.
  4. Track how much caffeine you have had in the day.

How much caffeine

Green tea and matcha contain both caffeine and L-theanine. A review (Nutrition Reviews 2008) describes their effects on mood and attention; we do not promise a specific benefit. EFSA limit at one time: 200 mg.

Tips

  • If you have started cutting coffee, matcha may be too strong: choose green tea.
  • A ritual works when it repeats: same cup, same place.

FAQ

Which tea suits focus?

Green tea and matcha have a moderate amount of caffeine and L-theanine; the effect is individual.

How many cups in a workday?

It depends on your caffeine budget: the calculator shows the daily total against the EFSA 400 mg.
