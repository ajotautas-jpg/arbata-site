Tea for guests instead of coffee: how to offer a choice

Offer 3–4 teas: one with more caffeine (black 14–48 mg), one lighter (green 11–29 mg) and one without (rooibos, herbal). In the evening offer the caffeine-free ones first.

What you need

  • 3–4 kinds of tea
  • Hot water and cups
  • A small card with caffeine amounts (optional)

How to make it

  1. Pick black, green, rooibos and one herbal blend.
  2. Put milk or a plant drink, lemon and honey nearby.
  3. Say which teas have no caffeine.
  4. After dinner offer the caffeine-free drinks first.

How much caffeine

Black 14–48 mg, green 11–29 mg, matcha 38–89 mg, a cup of filter coffee 80–107 mg. Rooibos has no caffeine.

Tips

  • You need not explain why no more coffee: just offer.
  • For pregnant guests EFSA suggests up to 200 mg of caffeine a day, so a caffeine-free choice is handy.

FAQ

What do I offer guests in the evening?

Rooibos, herbal tea or rooibos latte, because they contain no caffeine.

How many kinds are enough?

3–4: one stronger, one lighter, one caffeine-free.
