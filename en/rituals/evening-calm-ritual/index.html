An evening calm ritual with tea

In the evening pick a warm caffeine-free drink (rooibos, herbal tea), the same cup and 10 minutes without a screen. Caffeine has a half-life of 3–7 hours, so have your last caffeinated cup much earlier.

What you need

  • Rooibos or herbal tea
  • A cup you like
  • 10 minutes in the evening without a screen

How to make it

  1. Choose a caffeine-free drink: rooibos or herbal tea (check the pack).
  2. Brew it and wait without your phone; making the tea is part of the ritual.
  3. Before the first sip take 3–5 slow breaths in and out.
  4. Drink slowly, at the same time each day.

How much caffeine

Rooibos contains no caffeine and most herbal teas do not either, but not all, so check the pack. Black tea has 14–48 mg per cup, green 11–29 mg.

Tips

  • This is a ritual idea, not a treatment: if anxiety or sleep problems persist, see a doctor.
  • Rooibos latte works well when you want a warm “coffee” in the evening.

FAQ

Does tea help you calm down?

We write about a ritual (a warm drink, a pause), not a medical effect. Teas with caffeine can interfere in the evening, so choose caffeine-free.

When should I have my last caffeinated cup?

Caffeine stays for a half-life of 3–7 hours, so for many people earlier is better. The last-cup tool helps estimate.
