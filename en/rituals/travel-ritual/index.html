A travel ritual: tea on the road instead of a coffee stop

Take a flask, a few tea bags and, if you like, a bottle of cold brew tea. That way you drink your own tea rather than coffee from a stop. Keep to no more than 200 mg of caffeine at one time (EFSA).

What you need

  • A flask or bottle
  • Tea bags (black, green and one caffeine-free)
  • Cold brew tea from the night before (optional)

How to make it

  1. Before the trip make cold brew tea and pour it into a bottle; keep it cold.
  2. Pack 3–4 bags for different needs.
  3. At a stop ask for hot water and brew your own tea.
  4. Note how much caffeine you have had (caffeine tracker).

How much caffeine

Black tea 14–48 mg, green 11–29 mg, a cup of filter coffee 80–107 mg. EFSA: up to 200 mg at one time and 400 mg a day for adults.

Tips

  • Hot water is often available free or for a small fee, but it depends on the place.
  • Keep cold brew cold and finish it within a day or two.

FAQ

Can I drink tea on the road instead of coffee?

Yes, a flask or hot water from a stop works. Caffeine varies, so choose by plan.

How much caffeine is safe at once?

EFSA: up to 200 mg at one time for adults.
