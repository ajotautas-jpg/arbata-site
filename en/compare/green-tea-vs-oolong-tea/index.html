Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Green tea or Oolong tea?

Green tea and oolong tea have similar caffeine per cup (a difference of under 20%).

How many cups of oolong tea equal one cup of green tea?

Using average values, one cup of green tea (20 mg) equals about 1.2 cups of oolong tea (17 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Oolong tea has less caffeine per cup (about 17 mg versus about 20 mg for green tea). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
