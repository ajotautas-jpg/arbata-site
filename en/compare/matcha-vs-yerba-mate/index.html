Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Matcha or Yerba mate?

Matcha and yerba mate have similar caffeine per cup (a difference of under 20%).

How many cups of yerba mate equal one cup of matcha?

Using average values, one cup of matcha (63 mg) equals about 0.9 cups of yerba mate (67 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Matcha has less caffeine per cup (about 63 mg versus about 67 mg for yerba mate). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
