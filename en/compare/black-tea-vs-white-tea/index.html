Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Black tea or White tea?

A cup of black tea has on average about 1.6× more caffeine than white tea. The ranges overlap, so in a particular cup the difference can go the other way.

How many cups of white tea equal one cup of black tea?

Using average values, one cup of black tea (31 mg) equals about 1.6 cups of white tea (19 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

White tea has less caffeine per cup (about 19 mg versus about 31 mg for black tea). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
