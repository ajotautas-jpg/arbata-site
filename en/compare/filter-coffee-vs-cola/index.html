Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Filter coffee or Cola?

A cup of filter coffee has on average about 2.9× more caffeine than cola.

How many cups of cola equal one cup of filter coffee?

Using average values, one cup of filter coffee (96 mg) equals about 2.9 cups of cola (33 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Cola has less caffeine per cup (about 33 mg versus about 96 mg for filter coffee). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
