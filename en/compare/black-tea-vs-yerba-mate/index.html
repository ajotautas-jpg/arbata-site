Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Black tea or Yerba mate?

A cup of yerba mate has on average about 2.2× more caffeine than black tea.

How many cups of yerba mate equal one cup of black tea?

Using average values, one cup of black tea (31 mg) equals about 0.5 cups of yerba mate (67 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Black tea has less caffeine per cup (about 31 mg versus about 67 mg for yerba mate). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
