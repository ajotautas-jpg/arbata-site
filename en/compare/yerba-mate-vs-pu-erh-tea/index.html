Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Yerba mate or Pu-erh tea?

A cup of yerba mate has on average about 2.5× more caffeine than pu-erh tea.

How many cups of pu-erh tea equal one cup of yerba mate?

Using average values, one cup of yerba mate (67 mg) equals about 2.5 cups of pu-erh tea (27 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Pu-erh tea has less caffeine per cup (about 27 mg versus about 67 mg for yerba mate). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
