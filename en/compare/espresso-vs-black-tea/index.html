Espresso vs Black tea: which has more caffeine?

A cup of espresso has on average about 2× more caffeine than black tea.

Per 100 ml: espresso has about 16.1× more caffeine than black tea.

The ranges overlap, so in a particular cup the difference can go the other way.

Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Espresso or Black tea?

A cup of espresso has on average about 2× more caffeine than black tea. The ranges overlap, so in a particular cup the difference can go the other way.

How many cups of black tea equal one cup of espresso?

Using average values, one cup of espresso (63 mg) equals about 2 cups of black tea (31 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Black tea has less caffeine per cup (about 31 mg versus about 63 mg for espresso). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
