Espresso vs Matcha: which has more caffeine?

Espresso and matcha have similar caffeine per cup (a difference of under 20%).

Per 100 ml: espresso has about 6.7× more caffeine than matcha.

Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Espresso or Matcha?

Espresso and matcha have similar caffeine per cup (a difference of under 20%).

How many cups of matcha equal one cup of espresso?

Using average values, one cup of espresso (63 mg) equals about 1 cups of matcha (63 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Matcha has less caffeine per cup (about 63 mg versus about 63 mg for espresso). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
