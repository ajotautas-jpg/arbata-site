Frequently asked questions

Which has more caffeine: Filter coffee or Energy drink?

A cup of filter coffee has on average about 1.2× more caffeine than energy drink.

How many cups of energy drink equal one cup of filter coffee?

Using average values, one cup of filter coffee (96 mg) equals about 1.2 cups of energy drink (79 mg). This is a guide, since both drinks vary by batch and preparation.

Which is better in the evening?

Energy drink has less caffeine per cup (about 79 mg versus about 96 mg for filter coffee). After 6 hours roughly half to a third of what you drank remains, but sensitivity varies a lot.
